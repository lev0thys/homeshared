homeshared makes household life easier:

• **Shared shopping list** — everyone adds items, check off at the store, products sync to the virtual fridge.
• **Group fridge** — see what you have, expiry dates and meal-plan reservations.
• **Recipes & meal plan** — suggestions from your fridge, weekly meals, add missing ingredients in one tap.
• **Household tasks** — split chores (optional per group).

Create a personal space or a family / flatmate group, invite members, and stay in sync in real time.
